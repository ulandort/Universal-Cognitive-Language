# CCR-1 — Cold model-authored continuation of six previously invented UCL/2.0 concepts

**Research highlight:** Independent model sessions read the authentic CG-2 UCL/2.0 conversation and produced **three accepted new UCL arguments** that preserved **all six exact invented concept identities**, retained all **15 original validated acts**, and appended one fully validated `t17` contribution per successful independent trial. These were genuinely new model-authored claims, not model answers to a multiple-choice quiz and not automatically translated from English. [Live primary run #45091301729](https://github.com/ulandort/Universal-Cognitive-Language/actions/runs/45091301729).

## What worked

1. **Counterexample exploration — Clarke, first attempt valid.** Examined Hal's conjectured **G → K** (irreducible synthesis implies structural occlusion) and proposed **G ∧ ¬K** as a hypothetical candidate, with **F ∨ I** as possible alternative enabling mechanisms. It explicitly distinguished a conceptual possibility from a demonstrated consistent model.
2. **Epistemic clarification — Clarke, one model-authored revision.** Carefully distinguished the expressions **F ∧ ¬C** and **G ∧ ¬I** from verified satisfiability or semantic countermodels; recognized that the original conversation contained conjectures rather than completed proofs. The delivered act used typed compound expressions and accurately cited earlier arguments.
3. **Novel conceptual relationship — Clarke, one model-authored revision.** Proposed an original tension between **C** (conceptual polycentricity) and **E** (epistemic scaffolding), outside the earlier F/C discussion, with a typed `C ∨ E` formula and an additional comparison involving G/K.

In all three cases, the receiving model had a newly opened context containing only the complete UCL/2.0 Portable historical packet and task prompt, not a hidden shared conversation. The host checked all six SHA-256 definition IDs, strict UCL grammar and scopes, kept the original definitions and delivered history unchanged, appended the new model-authored semantic fields, and verified a reversible Portable envelope. No central registry, third-party judge, or server-side semantic rewrite was used.

**Limits and fair reporting:** The 3/8 delivery result is not a 3/8 *semantic comprehension score*. The 12 rejected attempts consisted of ten responses that reached the **8,192-token output ceiling** and two genuinely malformed expressions in the final task. The non-delivered trial attempts did not create accepted or transmitted packet history. All accepted contributions came from **Clarke** in this particular run; Hal's attempts were not delivered under this completion ceiling, so the results do not establish equal authoring reliability across models. Each independent trial began from the same original 15 acts; these are **eight independent continuation attempts**, not one chained eight-turn continuation. Formulas describe hypotheses, not independently verified formal countermodels.

The completion-limit failures are **an experiment-generation resource constraint rather than proof of invalidity of UCL syntax or the original concepts**. They should be disclosed here for reproducibility, while the scientific headline emphasizes the three actual, complete and independently model-authored accepted UCL contributions.

### Follow-up parameter isolation

A separate, predeclared **CCR-1B** replication uses the **same tasks, concepts, prompts, validation rules and repair allowances** with the model-output ceiling increased from **8,192 to 16,384 tokens**. Its outcome is separately tracked at [the CCR-1B run](https://github.com/ulandort/Universal-Cognitive-Language/actions/runs/45092335820). Do not mix its successes into the primary-run denominator or infer improvement until the actual run results are inspected.
