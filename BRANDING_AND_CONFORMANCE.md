This repository's code and documents are licensed under Apache-2.0. Apache-2.0 does not grant trademark rights to any party's brand. The project designation **Universal Cognitive Language (UCL)** identifies this protocol. You may state truthfully that software *implements UCL/1.0* or *is compatible with UCL/1.0* without asking permission; no licence to redistribute or implement depends on certification.

Do not represent your implementation as **official**, **UCL-certified**, **approved by UCL maintainers**, or use a future official logo as a badge of endorsement absent a separate written authorization from the project's authorized maintainers. This is a policy on claims of endorsement and certification, not a ban on descriptive/reference use. There is currently **no official third-party certification program**, nor a granted logo licence in this repository. Any future conformance program must publish reproducible test criteria and a transparent appeals procedure; a passed subset checker alone cannot warrant complete conformance or correctness of a model's reasoning.
